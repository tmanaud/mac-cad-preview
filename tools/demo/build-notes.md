# Gear pump: build notes

Second prototype of the bench gear pump. **Body milled from 6061**, gears printed, shafts from 12 mm ground stock.

## Parts

1. Pump body, 160 × 100 × 30 mm
2. Drive gear, 24 teeth, module 3
3. Idler gear, 16 teeth, module 3
4. Two shafts and six cover screws

## Before the next print

- Check the backlash at 60 mm between centres
- Add a *0.5 mm chamfer* to every tooth
- ~~Reprint the idler gear~~ done

> Clamp the body on parallels, not on the vise jaws, or the chamber floor will not be flat.

```
layer height   0.2 mm
infill         40 % gyroid
perimeters     4
```
